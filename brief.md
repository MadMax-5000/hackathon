# C03: We found the tyre issue and still never made the offer

Day 1 / Octopus.

## Your client

Tyre and service coordinator. The following is fictional role-play dialogue, not a real client quote.

> We inspect stored wheels, but the useful information does not reliably become a customer conversation before the next appointment. Sometimes the record is incomplete. Sometimes the proposed offer is for the wrong quantity.

## Your starting question

How can an observation become the right proposed offer, with a clear path for incomplete records?

You have `initial.json`. Ask the client about the workflow before deciding what to build.

## Working agreement

This is a fictional exercise inspired by real workflow patterns. The records, dialogue and rules are synthetic. You are free to challenge the first proposed solution. Discover who needs what decision, then choose a narrow workflow.

Use the harness and tools provided by the event. Tool access must not become the task. If a service is unavailable, use a labeled local simulation. Disclose generated or hard-coded outputs. A scripted animation is not evidence that an agent performed an action.

Expect a change of information during the exercise. Show a trigger, an evidence-backed proposal, a person who can approve or correct it, and the resulting state. A button that explicitly simulates an incoming event is acceptable. Call it a simulation.

## What to hand over

- A working prototype or clearly labeled clickable demonstration with a repeatable start state.
- A short record of the pain, client feedback and what you changed after feedback.
- An evidence view and at least one uncertain or failure case.
- A short README: run instructions, real vs simulated components, limitations and the next validation test.
- A Wolf handoff identifying the next integration, access requirements, owner and unresolved risk.

Show the result first in your presentation. State which promise you can demonstrate and which remains a hypothesis. Do not contact real customers or publish your prototype as an official client service.

## Exercise boundary

Condition labels, stock, appointments and customers are synthetic. There are no live prices or supplier orders. This exercise is not tyre safety advice. Suitability is a named human decision.

Next action: interview your client and agree on one observable outcome.
